## Concrete modeset mismatch

The compositor log chooses slot1/CRTC69 for USB-1, leaving slot2/CRTC88
unused. All ATOMIC_TEST_ONLY modesets fail with EINVAL before a hardware
modeset. A read-only `modetest -M apple -e` reports encoder91 (USB-1)
possible_crtcs=0x4, which allows slot2/CRTC88, not the chosen slot1/CRTC69.

The experimental driver advertises both external CRTCs at registration,
then narrows the encoder mask after selecting dcpext1 at hotplug. Its comment
assumes userspace re-reads that mask, but the observed compositor does not.
Aquamarine's primary source sets possibleCrtcs in SDRMConnector::init:
https://github.com/hyprwm/aquamarine/blob/main/src/backend/drm/DRM.cpp
That corroborates the observed stale allocation; the live logs and encoder
query are the machine-specific evidence. No compositor/config change is
needed to demonstrate the mismatch, and none was made.
