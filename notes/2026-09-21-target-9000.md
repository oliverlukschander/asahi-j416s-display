Hyprland: eDP-1 only. Hub up. No reboot loop.

DPTX validate target=0x9000 core=0 atc=2 die=0

Firmware accepted the remote-port, then had no DPTX object. Same as
`0x8020`/`0x9020`. ATC=0 + DPIN field does not create a device.

Closed with PHY 3 (HDMI jack) and ATC 2 (USB4): DCP firmware has no
DPTX object for USB-C dpin analog. Remote-port bits cannot invent one.
