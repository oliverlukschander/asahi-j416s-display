# USB4 dock display — where we are (2026-09-21)

## Why eDP goes black

`phy@39c000000` (lpdptxphy) `core+0x10` is a DCP-index mux. Writing **2**
(dcpext1) makes firmware train HBR3 / 4 lanes on the USB-C DPTX **and**
takes that engine away from the laptop panel. Analog bring-up is not
required. DRM hotplug is not required. `assign_only` (0042) proved it:

```
assign_only core+0x10 0 -> 2
SET_LINK_RATE 0x1e
SET_ACTIVE_LANE_COUNT 4
cb_hotplug ignored
eDP black
```

A second `ioremap` of the same PHY hangs the SoC (0040). Full
`phy_set_mode(DP)` analog activate also blanks eDP (0035/0037).

## What already works (this cabling)

- USB4 hub 40 Gb/s, keyboard, tunnel `0:5 ↔ 1:19` VE=AE=HPD=1
- T602x dpin0 analog mux to `dispext1`
- Keep-tunnel on DPRX timeout
- **DPTX training is real** when lpdptxphy is assigned to dcpext

## What does not work together

Laptop panel **and** that DPTX engine at the same time. ATC 2 in USB4
has no firmware DPTX object. HDMI PHY 3 is the empty jack (PS190).

## Gap to a lit VMM7100 with eDP still on

AUX/video into DP IN `0:5` without stealing `core+0x10` from eDP.
0085 booted: `set_hpd` after the nub returned 0, but only after
`DCPDPDevice` had already hit its 5 s start timer (`0xe0115022`,
22/24, detach). Analog `+0x18=0x16`. No `SET_LINK_RATE`. DPRX=0.
eDP stayed on. The 1 s AFK abort was not the cause. The PHY
behind that device does not answer.
Best waiting state remains 0079/0083: `0x9001` unit 0, unk
`0x100`, set_hpd only before the nub, no ATC AUX, analog
`0x1017`. 0083 `0x050` does not stick.
0082 firmware powered nub `0x25` then waited. 0081 ATC AUX closed.
0080 `unk=0x101` closed. 0078 hub-from-init blanked eDP — plug the
hub only after eDP-1 is up.
Auto-continue disarmed. Do not bind ATC=4 or unit 1. Do not pulse
analog. Do not `set_hpd` after request_display. Do not request_display
on `0x9000`. Do not assign lpdptxphy core+0x10. Do not `phy_set_mode(DP)`
the USB4 SS lanes. Do not enable ATC AUX on the 0x9001 skip path.
