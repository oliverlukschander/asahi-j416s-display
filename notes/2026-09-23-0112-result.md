## What this establishes

secondary_bit=0 (mode_value=8, 0111), secondary_bit=1 (mode_value=9,
0110), secondary_bit=2 (mode_value=10, 0112) -- have now been tested and
all three run cleanly with no picture. This is a materially stronger
signal than any single candidate alone: assuming the static analysis's
"3-valid-value enumeration" bound for that field was itself correct (it
was independently confirmed, but is still a reading of an unlabeled
enum, not a documented one), the free parameter's entire plausible
range is exhausted. That shifts the balance of evidence from "the guess
was numerically wrong" toward "this MODE_A/MODE_B write pair, at least
in the form written here, is not what's blocking the picture" -- without
being able to fully rule out a formula error elsewhere (e.g. the
enumeration itself having more than 3 valid values, or the multiplicative
combination with rate_class being wrong).
