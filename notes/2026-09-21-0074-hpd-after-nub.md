# 0074 set_hpd after request_display

0073: CORE=1 `GET_SUPPORTS_HPD=1`, nub + ACTIVATE, no 22/24.
Analog `+0x18=0x1017`. `set_hpd` ran before the nub was powered,
so firmware sat waiting for HPD.

Move `set_hpd(true)` to after `request_display` returns (ACTIVATE
already ran). That is the rising edge the HPD path needs.

Boot: `set_hpd after request_display: -110` (~1 s AFK timeout). Then
APCALL 22/24. Analog `+0x18=0x16`. DPRX=0.

Closed: do not call `set_hpd` after `request_display`. Firmware's
DPTX service does not answer AFK 8,8 once the nub is powered.

0075: restore 0073 order (`set_hpd` before `request_display`) and
pulse ACIO analog `+0x00` on ACTIVATE.
