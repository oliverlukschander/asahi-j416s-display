# 0074 set_hpd after request_display

0073: CORE=1 `GET_SUPPORTS_HPD=1`, nub + ACTIVATE, no 22/24.
Analog `+0x18=0x1017`. `set_hpd` ran before the nub was powered,
so firmware sat waiting for HPD.

Move `set_hpd(true)` to after `request_display` returns (ACTIVATE
already ran). That is the rising edge the HPD path needs.

Look for `set_hpd after request_display: 0`, then `SET_LINK_RATE` /
`SET_ACTIVE_LANE_COUNT` / 22/24 / DPRX.
