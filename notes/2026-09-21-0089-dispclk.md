# 0089 copy panel disp-1 onto dcpext1

`appledrm.usb4_dispclk=1` maps the panel block `0x389320000` and
the external block `0x315320000` (16 KB each). Words that are
non-zero on the panel and zero on dcpext1 are copied. Then dpin
is reselected and HPD is asserted. Not called at boot.

Watch `USB4 dispclk copied`, `t602x dpin0 clk` `800=`,
`SET_LINK_RATE`, and a second monitor. eDP-1 must stay on.
