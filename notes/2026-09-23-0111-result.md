One capture artifact worth noting for the record: the kernel also logs an
unrelated "ACIO RC dpin0 analog DPRX done" register dump at this same
boot. Its offsets 0x14/0x18/0x1c numerically coincide with our own
MODE_A/MODE_B offsets but are a different register window entirely --
`apple_dp_dump_analog()` reads from `acio->rc_base + APPLE_CIO_DPIN0_ANALOG`
(the ACIO root-complex analog capability block), not the
`0xf01e50000`-based DPIN0 handshake block our code writes
(`thunderbolt-apple-acio f01ac0000.cio: native DPIN0: active=1
base=0x0000000f01e50000`). Confirmed by reading
`apple_dp_dump_rc_range()`/`apple_dp_dump_analog()` in apple.c. This dump
does not reflect our MODE_A/MODE_B writes and should not be used to
infer anything about them.
