## Two misleading diagnostics

The probe's final lanes=4 is NOT proof of training. GET_MAX_LANE_COUNT in
dptxep.c assigns dptx->lane_count=4 before firmware requests any active lanes.
Use SET_ACTIVE_LANE_COUNT plus link rate, DPRX and the actual display result
as evidence, not this cached field. This misleading field was introduced in
the new probe diagnostic; it must not be propagated as a successful result.

The fault log's target 0:4 comes from dcp->dptx_phy (the old route metadata).
The actual request log confirms target 0x8001, core 1, ATC 0, die 0. Do not
infer a physical PHY 4 assignment from the fault message. The probe refused
any non-null dptx->atcphy, and no physical PHY was assigned by it.
