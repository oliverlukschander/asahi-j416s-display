# 0076 CORE=1 ATC=4 (0x9041) without lpdptxphy steal

0075 closed: ACTIVATE analog pulse, writes bounce, `+0x18` consumed.
0073 handshake (set_hpd before nub, GET_SUPPORTS_HPD=1) still avoids
22/24. Firmware has a DPTX object for ATC=4 (lpdptxphy). CORE=1 is
the USB4 dpin0 input.

Bind `0x9041` (CORE=1 ATC=4 DPIN=1). Do not `phy_set_mode`, do not
write `core+0x10`. `usb4_atc=0` rolls back to `0x9001`.

Boot: `0x9041` validate/connect/set_hpd 0. Same nub `0x854e10`.
APCALL 18, 10, then **APCALL 1 DEACTIVATE** (0073 was ACTIVATE).
eDP on. Analog `+0x18=0x1017`. DPRX=0.

Closed: ATC=4 is the eDP engine. Firmware DEACTIVATEs it for USB4
without `core+0x10=2`. CORE=1 ATC=0 (0073) ACTIVATEs.
